# PRODUCT REQUIREMENT DOCUMENT (PRD)

# Bakery ERP System

Version: 1.3.0

Status: Draft

Prepared By: Product Owner

Methodology: Agile Scrum

Target Go-Live: 2 Months

---

# 1. Product Vision

Membangun sistem ERP Bakery yang terintegrasi untuk mengelola proses:

* Penjualan (POS)
* Inventory
* Produksi
* Distribusi
* Replenishment
* Dashboard

dengan tujuan meningkatkan efisiensi operasional dan menyediakan data bisnis secara real-time.

---

# 2. User Persona

## Owner (Super Admin)

### Goals

* Melihat seluruh operasional bisnis.
* Memantau penjualan.
* Memantau stok.
* Mengelola produksi.
* Mengelola outlet.

### Pain Points

* Laporan manual.
* Sulit memantau stok.
* Sulit memantau produksi.

---

## Supervisor Outlet

### Goals

* Memastikan stok outlet tersedia.
* Membuat permintaan stok.
* Memantau penjualan outlet.

---

## Kasir

### Goals

* Melakukan transaksi dengan cepat.
* Mencetak struk.

---

## Admin Gudang

### Goals

* Mengelola stok.
* Mengelola transfer.
* Mengelola batch.

# 5.3 Product Management

## Features

---

## Product Type

* RAW_MATERIAL
* FINISHED_GOODS
* PACKAGING

---

## Fields

* SKU
* Barcode
* Name
* Product Group
* Category
* Unit
* Supplier
* Purchase Price
* Selling Price
* Minimum Stock
* Shelf Life Days
* Batch Tracking


---

## Acceptance Criteria

* SKU unik.
* Product type wajib.
* Shelf life wajib.
* Batch tracking default aktif.
* Setiap produk wajib terhubung dengan Product Group, Category, UOM (Unit), dan Supplier.
* Validasi Category harus sesuai dengan Product Group yang dipilih (misal, kategori tepung harus berada di bawah Product Group RAW_MATERIAL).

## Objective

Mengelola stok seluruh gudang.

---

## Features

* View stock by expiry.

---

### Inventory Movement

* View transaction source.

---

### Stock Adjustment

* Positive Adjustment.
* Negative Adjustment.

---

### Stock Opname

* Full Count.

# 7. Production Module

## Objective

Mengelola proses produksi bakery.

---

## Features

### Recipe/BOM

* Create Recipe.
* Versioning.
* Activate Recipe.

---

### Production Order

* Create Production Order.
* Start Production.
* Complete Production.

---

### Production Waste

* Record Waste.

---

## Business Rules

* FEFO wajib.
* Batch expired tidak dapat digunakan.
* Produksi menghasilkan batch baru.
* Approval tidak diperlukan.

---

## Production Flow

1. Create Production Order.
2. Select Recipe.
3. Input Planned Quantity.
4. System calculates material requirement.
5. System allocates FEFO batch.
6. Start Production.
7. Complete Production.
8. Generate Finished Goods Batch.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

* Sistem mengurangi bahan baku otomatis.
* Sistem menambah produk jadi otomatis.
* Waste tercatat.

---

## Screens

* Recipe List
* Recipe Form
* Production List
* Production Form
* Production Detail

---
