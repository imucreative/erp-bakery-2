Diminta PM untuk mengeksekusi **POS/Sales Fase 1: modul Shift kasir (backend)** di branch
`feature/pos-sales`, acuan desain APPROVED `51.pos-sales-design.md` (§4.2 Shift, §6.2 lifecycle,
§9 permission, §10 API). Scope dibatasi HANYA Shift — tanpa sales order/payment/checkout (itu Fase 2).

Sebelum menulis kode, saya menelaah modul paling matang bergaya dokumen-lifecycle sebagai acuan:
Stock Opname (`StockOpnameRepositoryPostgres`, `opnameStatus.ts`, controller + use case + DTO),

mempelajari wiring DI di `InfrastructureModule.ts`, registrasi module di `app.module.ts`,
`DomainErrorTranslator.ts`, helper `successResponse/listResponse` + `parsePagination`, decorator
`@RequirePermission`/`@GetUser`, dan gaya unit test (mocked `pg` Pool/Client — murni unit, tanpa DB).

Yang dikerjakan:

  cashier_user_id, opened_at, closed_at, opening_cash, expected_cash, actual_cash, discrepancy,
  status OPEN/CLOSED (CHECK), notes, audit fields doc 35 §4 + updated_by + closed_by). **Partial
  unique index** `shifts_one_open_per_cashier_outlet ON (cashier_user_id, outlet_id) WHERE
