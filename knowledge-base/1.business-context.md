# Business Context

## Project Information

Project Name: Bakery ERP

Industry: Bakery Retail & Manufacturing

Project Type: ERP Lite / POS + Inventory + Production

Methodology: Agile Scrum

Target Go-Live: 2 Months

Architecture Style: Modular Monolith

---

# Business Objectives

The system is built to:

1. Replace manual Excel processes.
2. Provide real-time inventory visibility.
3. Manage bakery production process.
4. Manage multi-outlet operation.
5. Reduce stock discrepancy.
6. Improve operational efficiency.
7. Provide management dashboard.

---

# Business Domain

Bakery business consists of:

* Central Outlet (head office / gudang pusat)
* Outlet (each outlet has its own stock location)
* Production Process
* Distribution Process
* Sales Process

---

# Outlet Model

The company uses Outlet-based inventory architecture (SaaS multi-tenant).

## Central Outlet

Responsibilities:

* Receive purchase from supplier.
* Store raw materials.
* Execute production.
* Transfer goods to outlets.

## Outlet

Responsibilities:

* Store finished goods.
* Sell products.
* Execute local production if needed.
* Request stock to Central Outlet.

---

# Production Model

Production can be executed in:

* Central Outlet.
* Outlet.

Production approval is NOT required.

---

# Inventory Rules

Inventory method uses:

FEFO (First Expired First Out)

System must always consume stock with nearest expiry date first.

---

# Batch Rules

All products must use batch tracking.

Applies to:

* Raw Material.
* Finished Goods.
* Semi Finished Goods.

Each batch contains:

* Batch Number.
* Expired Date.
* Available Quantity.

---

# Expiry Rules

Expired products cannot:

* Be sold.
* Be transferred.
* Be consumed in production.

System must provide expiry alert.
